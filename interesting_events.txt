You can use SEQUOIA for running some evebts
I higly suggest using dansur as is the fastest surrogate model.
You must write the path to your data folder and where do you want your results in config.py. You can also change your surrogate model there. 
You can change the number of threads used by changing nparalle and npool, located in run_inference_bilby. This will be helpful for nrsur surrogate as it is computationally expensive due to the inclusion of precessiong effects.

I will briefly discuss the characteristics of the events I have uploaded:

-GW150914: This one was the first event ever detected.

-GW200208_130117: average LVK detections.

-GW230825_041334:  high spin bynaty system.

-GW190701_203306: high mass event.	 (approximate total mass:  94,3 +12 -9.5 Solar masses.)

-GW231119_075248: a very far away bynary system (approximate luminosty distance: 6700 + 3000 - 2500 Mpc, remeber 1 Mpc = 3,262*10^6 light years. )

You can find more information about these and more events at: https://gwosc.org/eventapi/html/GWTC/
